# Testing rules

## Guarantee and proof

**Invariant:** identify a new/changed Test's Intent (guarantee and distinguishing
defect), production Seam, independent Oracle, required Environment and Requirement
Origin. Origins include accepted Scenario, historical regression, domain invariant,
support claim, interoperability specification or budget decision; an ID locates
but does not establish origin. Intents are Behavior, Invariant, Conformance and
Budget; Vector is a proof form. Replacement preserves historical failure detection.
**Default:** recover these facts from requirement, declaration, body and configuration;
explain non-obvious choices/gaps without mandatory metadata or per-Test tables.
Plan sufficient evidence and its limits, not Test counts.

## Select complete owners

Read every applicable owner completely; several rows can apply. These routes refine
Testing only. Retain other applicable and local requirements. Establish unknown facts
at their narrow owner before deciding to skip; reclassify newly discovered consequences.
An established unchanged dimension need not be redesigned merely to edit a title.

| Owner | Load for | Alone insufficient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Identity and navigation](testing/identity-navigation.md) | Test markers/titles/IDs, Check definitions, native grouping, declaration layout/collection, or recovery of full identity. | Body changes with established unchanged identity and organization. |
| [Proof and environments](testing/proof-environments.md) | Guarantee separation, seam/oracle/assertions/observations, interaction proof, Test preparation sufficiency, environment/support claims or evaluation of evidence sufficiency. | Human-only title with all five dimensions unchanged; locator replacement with unchanged semantic observation, readiness and proof sufficiency. |
| [Preparation, Cases and resources](testing/preparation-cases.md) | Test inputs, controlled dependency outcomes, Case data (including expectations), state preparation, fixture lifecycle/isolation, changed support responsibilities or authored/generated cases. | Unchanged data read only as a fact. |
| [Declarations and artifacts](testing/declarations-artifacts.md) | Proposing/evaluating special type evidence, consumer type usability, packed entries or package-artifact contract evidence. | Running an unchanged build; the word delivery without these claims. |
| [Representations](testing/representations.md) | Exact bytes/forms/identities, vector data/list/loaders/expectations, or visual snapshot baselines. | Equality without a representation contract; rendered UI without visual comparison evidence. |
| [Evidence lifecycle](testing/lifecycle.md) | Any direct or causal Test change, Scenario-tree change, evidence replacement/removal, Todo/skip/gaps, or evaluating required-proof completeness. | A Story with no changed evidence or claim to establish proof. |
| [Budgets and delivery](testing/budgets.md) | Measurement method/threshold, accepted budget, measurement outcome or delivering a candidate under a budget. | Timing an unrelated unchanged command without a budget/evidence task. |
| [UI support](testing/ui-support.md) | Selectors, Page Objects, Stories, rendering support or their environment/import boundaries. | A UI consumer, UI mention or headless Test without these responsibilities. |

Follow all consequences: new vector Tests also involve identity, authored inputs,
independent proof and lifecycle; changing state preparation needs proof sufficiency;
a Story is not automatically evidence. A selector change used by Tests reaches
lifecycle; changed observation or readiness adds proof or preparation as applicable.
