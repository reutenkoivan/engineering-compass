# Budgets and delivery

**Invariant:** accepted product budgets need reproducible automated evidence. For a
candidate being delivered/published: build/pack, measure under the agreed method and
environment, compare with the independently agreed threshold, deliver that candidate.
These are execution dependencies, not four mandatory scripts. Development diagnostics
do not activate delivery. `[b]` proves a production operation with representative
controlled input; artifact budgets may be Verification. Threshold-free work is diagnostic.

Evidence names candidate, method, actual conditions, value and comparison; existing
tools need no JSON reporter/registry. Noisy measurement is not a valid sole threshold
without a reliable agreed method. Exceeded and unmeasurable are non-passing. Predetermine
retries/aggregation/exclusions; no cherry-picking or erasing failure through diagnostic
reruns. Method/threshold changes need their owner's justification and do not fix candidates.
